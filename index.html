About Mashaq Interior

A Hyderabad studio built on craft, trust and detail

Founded by Managing Director Mahamd Rafiq and Executive Director Mashaq Patel, Mashaq Interior brings together factory engineering, traditional craftsmanship and technical design under one roof.

Our in-house team of designers, technical architects, factory engineers and site supervisors works from first concept to final handover — so your project is delivered by one trusted team, on the timeline we promise.
