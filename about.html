Company Introduction

Who We Are

Mashaq Interior is a full-service interior design and build studio based in Hyderabad. Led by Managing Director Mahamd Rafiq and Executive Director Mashaq Patel, we are a team of designers, technical architects, factory craftsmen and site supervisors who work together from first concept to final handover.

What We Do

We design and deliver residential and commercial interiors — modular kitchens, bedrooms, living rooms, wardrobes, false ceilings, woodwork, lighting and complete turnkey projects. Alongside interiors, we also take on renovation, civil work, plumbing, painting, fabrication and building supervision, so our clients have one trusted team for the entire journey.

Our Background

Mashaq Interior was founded with a simple belief: the quality of factory engineering and the warmth of traditional craftsmanship belong together. That belief has guided every project we've delivered — from a single modular kitchen to full villa turnkey interiors across Hyderabad.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted interior design studio in Hyderabad — known for interiors that stand the test of time, respect every client's budget and reflect the people who live in them. We want to raise the standard of what homeowners expect from a design partner: honesty, craftsmanship and genuine care, every step of the way.

02

Our Mission

To deliver quality designs through a transparent, end-to-end process — from the first consultation and 3D visualisation, to factory precision and on-site handover. We keep our materials honest, our timelines real, and our communication clear. Every project, regardless of size, gets the same attention to material, finish and detail.
